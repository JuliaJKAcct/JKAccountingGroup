# Social media posts (Instagram · Facebook)

The rules live in **[`projects/marketing/social-content-playbook.md`](../../../projects/marketing/social-content-playbook.md)** —
**read it before writing copy.** This file is the operating procedure: what to do,
in order, and what to say out loud while doing it.

The playbook was built from a personal-brand kit Julia brought in August 2026 and
adapted to a regulated accounting practice. It exists because these rules are easy
to agree with and easy to forget — **so state them as you apply them.** A session
that silently produces a good-looking post has not done the job.

## The procedure

1. **Read the playbook.** Then say, in one line, which **pillar** this post stands
   on (expertise · personality · proof) and which **mix bucket** it fills
   (useful 50% · personality 20% · trust 20% · offer 10%). If neither is
   answerable, the post has no reason to exist — fix that first, before writing.
   *(Two things this skill also covers are not single posts and skip this step:
   the **profile bio**, which answers to Rule 2 — the four elements legible to a
   first-time visitor — and a **content plan**, where the pillar and bucket are
   assigned per row and the mix is checked across the whole month.)*
2. **Name the one action** the reader should take: save · follow · comment ·
   click · **book a free discovery call**. One, not three. Write the post toward
   it. *(The CTA wording is fixed — "free discovery call", never "free
   consultation"; the consultation is paid, $150 / 1 hour. And verify the booking
   link opens before it goes into anything published.)*
3. **Check the month, not just the post.** If a content plan exists, say how the
   mix is running — and flag it when the **offer** slot is empty, because that is
   the one the firm skips. If no plan exists, offer to build one (topic · pillar ·
   bucket · action, 3–4 posts a week).
4. **Write it** — Julia's first-person voice, calm senior advisor, EN and RU each
   written natively. Verify every figure, deadline and threshold against an
   authoritative source (IRS first); cut what cannot be verified.
5. **Run the pre-publish checklist** in the playbook and report it. Then hand off
   to `social-post-workflow.md` for the visual and the network size.

## The lines that do not bend

- **No client data.** No client name, business, logo or figures — no screenshot of
  anyone's books — without explicit written permission. Publish the *shape* of a
  case, anonymized, never the person.
- **Real facts only.** No invented statistic, no fabricated testimonial, no AI or
  stock faces. If a number cannot be verified, it is cut.
- **Educational, not advice.** Tax content is general education, not a
  recommendation for the reader's situation.
- **No fear, no hype, no hard-sell.** The audience is anxious; we are the calm in
  the room. This is the brand guide's hardest rule and social is where it gets
  broken.
- **Exact text is placed by us, never typed by an image model.** Image AIs
  misspell and invent numbers — final text and figures go on in the brand finish.

## Remind, unprompted

Two things are worth saying even when nobody asked, because they are what this
skill is for:

- **When a post is requested with no plan behind it** — produce the post, and name
  the pillar, bucket and action anyway. Then say what the month is missing.
- **When several posts in a row come from the same bucket** — say so. Four useful
  posts and no proof is the drift the 50/20/20/10 mix exists to catch.
