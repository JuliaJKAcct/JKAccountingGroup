Fee basis

What this option changesactive — in the letter

When ON, the Professional Fee line reads “estimated at $X” (instead of a fixed “$X”), and this sentence is added right after it:

“Accordingly, the amount stated above is a good-faith estimate; the final fee may be adjusted to reflect the actual complexity of the return, and should the work require a material change from this estimate, we will review it with you and obtain your agreement before proceeding — you will not receive an unexpected invoice.”

Leave OFF for a fixed, final fee (the standard wording). Everything else in the letter stays the same.

“To reserve your place on our schedule and begin the work, we ask for a $X deposit, which is credited in full toward your final fee.”

and the Professional Fee section notes the balance — after the deposit — is invoiced on completion. Leave OFF for no deposit.
