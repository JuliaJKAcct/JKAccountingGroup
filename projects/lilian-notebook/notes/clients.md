# Clients & communication

How to write to them, what they have to do themselves, and what never goes out without
telling them first.

## LN-24 — Correct the draft; don't rewrite it. "Shorter" means fewer of *your* words
- **Tags:** writing · messages · russian
- **Certainty:** Firm rule
- **Star:** yes
- **Added:** 2026-08-06
- **Came from:** Ecoorganic / Turo, Aug 2026 — the message Lilian actually sent
- **Detail:** [`CLAUDE.md`](../../../CLAUDE.md) — the firm-wide rule

**What happened.** Lilian handed over a draft and asked for it shorter. The "shortened"
version came back rewritten in someone else's voice. The message she actually sent ended up
**~35% longer** than that — because she kept her own text and cut only what had been added to
it. The word count was never the problem.

**The rule.** The deliverable is **her** message with the mistakes repaired — her sentences,
her order of ideas, minimally edited. "Shorter" means cutting **clarifications, conditionals,
unsolicited advice, repetition and explanations of *why*.** Never cut: the greeting and
courtesy (in a client message that *is* the relationship, not filler), the sentence showing
the firm **already tried** (it's what justifies asking the client to do work), her phrasing,
or the actionable data. Format it for a phone: a number or code **on its own line with a
label** so it's tappable, one idea per block, and document names in **English, capitalized**
when the client will have to say them out loud.

## LN-25 — Some vendors will only talk to the client. Schedule the call, don't make it alone
- **Tags:** vendors · access · scheduling
- **Certainty:** Established
- **Star:** yes
- **Added:** 2026-08-06
- **Came from:** Turo, for Ecoorganic — two calls
- **Detail:** [`ecoorganic-usa.md` §3/§4](../../client-intelligence/clients/ecoorganic-usa.md)

**What happened.** Turo confirmed it *could* produce the documents, then refused to hand them
to Lilian — and refused even to email them to the address already on the account without the
owner calling himself. The second call, with the owner on the line, got the case escalated in
one pass.

**The rule.** With a consumer platform, **assume nothing moves without the account holder
present** and book the call with them from the start. Two calls, one of which achieves
nothing, is the cost of finding out the other way. Practical help: capture the phone-tree
sequence once and send it to the client with the exact words to ask for.

## LN-26 — If you haven't asked in writing, you haven't asked
- **Tags:** documents · chasing · evidence
- **Certainty:** Established
- **Star:** yes
- **Added:** 2026-08-06
- **Came from:** Gossip Miami — the incoming-check images
- **Detail:** [`gossip-miami.md` §6](../../client-intelligence/clients/gossip-miami.md)

**What happened.** The client verbally committed to going to the bank for check images —
both the checks the company wrote **and** the ones deposited into it. Reviewing the record
showed the **incoming half had never been requested in writing** at all. He was responding,
not stonewalling; the ask simply didn't exist.

**The rule.** Before chasing someone for the second time, **re-read what was actually sent.**
A verbal yes doesn't create a record, a written list does — and one written list beats four
verbal reminders. Second half of the same rule: **check what you can already see before you
chase.** The firm has user access to that client's QuickBooks, so whether a bank feed is
reconnected is verifiable without asking anyone.

## LN-27 — Never touch a client's registration or billing before telling them
- **Tags:** consent · billing · registrations
- **Certainty:** Firm rule
- **Star:** no
- **Added:** 2026-08-06
- **Came from:** Tsminibears (state registration) and Ecoorganic (QuickBooks plan)
- **Detail:** [`tsminibears.md` §4](../../client-intelligence/clients/tsminibears.md) · [`ecoorganic-usa.md` §3](../../client-intelligence/clients/ecoorganic-usa.md)

**What happened.** Two live examples in the same week: a plan to move a state account's
effective date on a former client's registration, and a subscription downgrade on a client's
own billing.

**The rule.** Changing a **state registration** or a **paid subscription** is the client's
decision even when it's obviously right and even when the firm caused the problem. Tell them
before, not after — and if it's a former client whose matter you're still resolving, tell
them anyway.
