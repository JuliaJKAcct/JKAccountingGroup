## LN-00 — The lesson, written as the rule, in one line
- **Tags:** two to four · lowercase · dot-separated
- **Certainty:** Established | Firm rule | Working assumption   ← exactly one of these three
                 (they render as a pill, so they stay short; any nuance goes in the body)
- **Star:** yes | no
- **Added:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Came from:** the client, matter or session that taught it — and the date
- **Detail:** where the full record lives (client file + section, a skill, a Double note ID)

**What happened.** Two to four sentences of the actual story — enough that the rule below
is obviously true, and enough to recognise the same situation next time. Concrete: names,
dates, amounts, who said what.

**The rule.** What to do differently. This is the part that shows without expanding
anything, so it has to stand alone. Imperative, specific, and if there's a cost or a
deadline attached, say it.
