> about where it goes**; appending to the end means the team never sees it. The cap lives in

- ⛔ **NEVER file another Form SS-4 for this entity.** The EIN (37-2243148) is assigned and
  the CP575B letter is in the firm's hands — Julia's explicit instruction, 2026-08-27. A
  second SS-4 is the classic way a new entity ends up with two EINs and a mess
- **All four members are Lithuanian NON-RESIDENTS (25% each)** — every filing runs through
  the foreign-partner machinery: none has an SSN/ITIN yet (hence the four W-7s, filed with
  the 1040-NRs), and ⚠️ **§1446 withholding (Forms 8804/8805) on income allocated to foreign
  partners is NOT in the quoted engagement** — flagged to Julia 2026-08-27, unresolved. If
  the property produces ECTI, the partnership has withholding obligations of its own
- **The engagement is conditional and first-year only:** it begins **after the property
  purchase closes** (not yet closed as of 2026-08-27). Tax year 2026 is assumed; if the
  purchase slips into 2027, the letter's year and dates must be redone before signing
- **Tax planning is EXCLUDED by the letter's standing terms** ($150/hr under a separate
  agreement) even though the intake asked for "tax preparation & tax planning" — flagged to
  Julia 2026-08-27; her call whether the standard text stands or planning gets its own line
- **The fee schedule is itemized per member by name** (Julia's instruction: no blanket
