## 5. Key facts & quirks

> ⚠️ Ordered by consequence — **only the first four are published** on the Hub and the review dashboard.

- 🔴 **His U.S. tax residency is not established, and two filings depend on it.** The 2024 Schedule G names him a **citizen of Ukraine** owning 100% of Wealth Bay, LLC, while Schedule K question 7 (foreign person owning ≥25%) was answered **No** and **no Form 5472 was filed**. He holds a Florida driving licence, which is not a residency test. If the "No" is wrong, Form 5472 was required — **$25,000 per form, per year.** Get the immigration status and the dates before preparing anything.
- 🔴 **An LLC filed Form 1120, and we have not seen the election that allows it.** Wealth Bay, LLC filed a corporate return. Without a **Form 8832** on file the default classification is disregarded or partnership, and the 2024 return is the wrong form. Ask for the election before touching 2025.
- 🔴 **The two 2024 entity returns contradict each other on who owns Rock Wealth LLC.** Its Schedule B-1 says **Wealth Bay LLC, a corporation, owns 99%**; the K-1 for that same 99% interest is issued to **Andrii personally with Wealth Bay marked as a disregarded entity.** Both cannot be true — Wealth Bay filed its own 1120, so it is not disregarded. The operating agreements settle it.
- 🔴 **Eight of eleven rental properties reported nothing at all in 2024**, and one lump of rent sits on a single property with zero expenses against it. Either eight properties' income and expenses were never entered, or the property list is wrong. Both readings change the passive-loss computation and the tax. **Only one of the eleven is being depreciated** — and depreciation is allowed *or allowable*, so basis is reduced either way.
- The **eight Forms 5471 were all filed under Category 1a** (section 965), with only one also ticking 5a. Six companies are held at exactly 50%, where CFC status turns on who owns the other half. Category cannot be fixed without the shareholder registers. All eight show an EIN of **"Applied For"** and carry no reference ID number.
- **Two Pennsylvania properties were bought and sold inside 2024** and reported as capital gains, while a Schedule C entity named *Wealth Forest, LLC — Flipping Real Estate* shows expenses and no revenue. Dealer versus investor has to be decided once and applied consistently; dealer means ordinary income plus self-employment tax.
- **Married filing separately, with a Marketplace premium tax credit.** An MFS filer is generally not eligible for the PTC at all; the 2024 return repaid the capped amount rather than the full advance credit. Confirm the status is deliberate and get the 2025 Form 1095-A early — it can decide the whole return.
- **Name collision:** *Avirock LLC* (Nevada, on Schedule C) and *Avirock Limited Liability Company* (Poland, on a Form 5471) are reported as two separate entities. Confirm whether one owns the other.
- **Three different addresses appear across one year of filings** — the 1040 header, a K-1, and the driving licence each give a different one. Establish the residence.
- **Foreign real estate reported on Form 8938** (agricultural land and a house with land). Directly-held foreign real estate is generally *not* a specified foreign financial asset. Either it sits inside one of the foreign companies — in which case the reportable asset is the stock — or it was over-reported.
