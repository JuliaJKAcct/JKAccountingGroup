A co-hosted podcast for business owners

Two advisors, one table — the questions your clients actually ask

A business attorney and an accountant answering real owner decisions together — because almost every one has both a legal side and a money side. One ~2-hour session becomes several episodes and a season of short clips.

Format

Video podcast

One session

~2 hours

Output

3–4 episodes

Audience

Business owners

Language

EN or RU

Your clients and mine are the same people, asking the same questions at the same moments — starting a company, hiring, signing a contract, opening a letter from the IRS. Most of those questions have a legal answer and a financial one, and today the owner gets only half of each. This podcast puts both in one conversation: you handle the law, I handle the money, and the owner finally hears the whole picture. It positions us both as the people to call — and sends work each other's way.

A few episodes we could open with

  • "Everyone told me to open an LLC — is that right?"Law: what it actually protects, and the operating agreement · Money: how it's taxed, and when to become an S-corp
  • "How to make your business hard to sue"Law: what pierces the corporate veil · Money: the clean books that keep that protection intact
  • "1099 or W-2 — am I classifying people right?"Law: what makes someone an employee · Money: what getting it wrong really costs
  • "Going into business with a partner"Law: operating agreements & the buy-sell · Money: draws, K-1s, and who owes what
  • "A letter came from the IRS"Law: your rights and representation · Money: what triggers it, and books as your defense
  • The immigrant founder's playbookLaw: entity & ownership for foreign founders · Money: US tax, ITIN, foreign-owned reporting

…and "the most expensive mistakes we both see" makes a great pilot.

Why it works for both of us

  • One audience, two experts — your clients need me, mine need you.
  • A warm, two-way referral engine — built right into the content.
  • Authority — together we become the owner's legal-and-financial brain trust.
  • Underserved niche — almost no quality content on US business law + tax for Russian-speaking founders.

What one shoot produces

3–4 full episodes — each a self-contained owner question, both sides answered.

15–20 short clips — one crisp answer each, for Reels / Shorts / LinkedIn: a full quarter of content.

A co-branded checklist — the "legal + financial setup" one-pager as a free lead magnet and a place to capture referrals.

The lift is small. We pick three topics, block a 2-hour session, and record — I'll bring the running order and the questions. If the idea resonates, I'll send a short list of episode topics and the exact questions we'd cover, and we find a date.
