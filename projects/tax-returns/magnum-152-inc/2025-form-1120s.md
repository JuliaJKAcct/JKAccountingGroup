## 0 · The year in one paragraph

Magnum 152 runs a multi-store Florida pawn group. **On 2025-05-21 it sold ONE of its stores** — the
`US Pawn Jewelry and Guns` location at 5450 South State Road 7, Ste 5 & 6 — to **Queen of Pawns &
Jewelry, Inc.** (d/b/a Queen of Pawns 16), EIN 65-0569810, for **$950,000**. 🔑 **This is an ASSET sale
of one location, not a sale of the company**: the corporation continues, the S election is untouched,
there is no short period, and **2025 is one ordinary full-year Form 1120-S** with a disposition inside it.
The corporation retained all cash, all bank accounts, all pre-closing receivables and payables, and
**everything firearms-related** — licenses, pawn contracts and the associated inventory.

⚠️ **Two structural features of the deal drive the whole return.** Half the price — **$475,000** — was
**not paid to the seller at closing**: it went to an escrow agent, to be released only when the buyer
obtained its own Florida pawn license and a City of Hollywood Business Tax Receipt. And under PSA
Article XI the **entire sale is rescindable** if the buyer never obtains them, with the seller repaying
everything received less $10,000. **Whether the escrow released, and in which tax year, is the single
biggest open question on this return.**

### 🔴 The principle that governs all of it

**THE CORPORATION'S AMOUNT REALISED IS 950,000.00 WHEREVER THE CASH WENT.** Magnum sold the assets, so
Magnum realised the whole price. The bank deposits are **not** the sale price and the gain cannot be
computed from them. The 313,400.00 has to land in one of exactly four buckets, and they are **not**
interchangeable:

| Bucket | Effect on the return |
|---|---|
| **Broker commission (Jerry Whitehead) · legal fees** | **Reduces the gain** — a selling expense, even though the cash never touched the company's bank |
| 🔴 **Wired to the shareholder personally** | ⛔ **Does NOT reduce the gain.** It is a **DISTRIBUTION** — Schedule K line 16d, K-1 box 16 code D, Schedule M-2, and it runs against his own stock basis under §1368 |
| **Still held in trust at 2025-12-31** | Gain is still 2025; the balance is a **receivable** on Schedule L |
| **Prorations under PSA §2.4** | Ordinary income/expense items, not part of the gain |
