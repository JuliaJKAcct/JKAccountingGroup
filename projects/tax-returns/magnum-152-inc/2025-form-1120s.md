## 0 · The year in one paragraph

Magnum 152 runs a multi-store Florida pawn group. **On 2025-05-21 it sold ONE of its stores** — the
`US Pawn Jewelry and Guns` location at 5450 South State Road 7, Ste 5 & 6 — to **Queen of Pawns &
Jewelry, Inc.** (d/b/a Queen of Pawns 16), EIN 65-0569810, for **$950,000**. 🔑 **This is an ASSET sale
of one location, not a sale of the company**: the corporation continues, the S election is untouched,
there is no short period, and **2025 is one ordinary full-year Form 1120-S** with a disposition inside it.
The corporation retained all cash, all bank accounts, all pre-closing receivables and payables, and
**everything firearms-related** — licenses, pawn contracts and the associated inventory.

⚠️ **Two structural features of the deal drive the whole return.** Half the price — **$475,000** — was
**not paid to the seller at closing**: it went to an escrow agent, to be released only when the buyer
obtained its own Florida pawn license and a City of Hollywood Business Tax Receipt. And under PSA
Article XI the **entire sale is rescindable** if the buyer never obtains them, with the seller repaying
everything received less $10,000. **Whether the escrow released, and in which tax year, is the single
biggest open question on this return.**

## 3 · 🔴 Findings on the documents as they stand

| # | Finding | Type | What it needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| **F1** | 🔴 **Form 8594 does not foot.** Class V reads **45,985.00** where the signed-by-nobody allocation schedule reads **45,895.00** — a transposed digit. The classes on the 8594 sum to **950,090.00** against a stated total of **950,000.00**. The schedule's own three figures do sum to 950,000.00, so **the schedule is right and the 8594 is wrong by $90** | 🔴 **DEFECT** | Correct Class V on the 8594 to **45,895.00** before it is attached. The form is a draft on an unfiled return, so this costs nothing now |
| **F2** | **8594 line 5 is answered `No`** — *no allocation agreed in writing signed by both parties*. But an allocation schedule exists, dated the day after closing, with signature blocks for both. **Our copy is unsigned.** PSA §2.5 says the parties *shall* allocate before the escrow releases, and that failing agreement **each side may allocate as it sees fit** | ⚖️ **DECISION** | Establish whether a **signed** copy exists. Signed → line 5 and its follow-up are both `Yes`. Unsigned → `No` stands, and then confirm the buyer used the same three numbers, because two different 8594s on one transaction is what the form exists to surface |
| **F3** | **8594 line 6 is answered `No`** — no license, covenant not to compete, lease, employment or management arrangement with the seller. But the purchased assets **include licenses**, the lease was **assigned**, PSA §3A.2 is a **non-solicitation covenant by the seller**, and PSA §11.1 has the seller's principal providing **post-closing transition assistance** and letting the buyer trade under the seller's own licence | ⚖️ **DECISION** | Julia's call. If any of those is treated as within line 6, the answer flips to `Yes` **and a statement must be attached** naming the arrangement and the maximum consideration |
| **F4** | 🔴 **The allocation gives equipment, security equipment and customer lists NOTHING.** All three transferred. The entire Class V amount is labelled `Leasehold Improvements` | 🔴 **AFFECTS THE FIGURES** | Under §1060 the Class V amount is spread across **all** Class V assets by relative FMV. Every Class V asset that transferred has to come off the books whatever it was allocated — assets allocated nothing produce a **loss**, and the split between them changes how much of the gain is **§1245 ordinary recapture** |
| **F5** | 🔴 **The escrow, and the timing of $475,000 of the price.** Release required a **written notice from the buyer**. Outside date ≈ 2025-11-21 | 🔴 **BLOCKS A FIGURE** | The release date and amount decide whether the whole $950,000 is 2025 income or whether the escrowed half is a contingent/installment amount. **Nothing on the return can be finalised without it** |
| **F6** | **The buyer traded at the location under MAGNUM's pawn licence and BTR after closing**, and PSA §11.1 says the seller is entitled to **no profits, fees, salary or other remuneration** for that period | 🔴 **AFFECTS THE FIGURES** | If any post-2025-05-21 activity at that store ran through Magnum's Bravo, merchant accounts or bank, it is **in Magnum's books and is not Magnum's income**. Must be identified and removed |
| **F7** | **Firearms were carved out.** The store's non-firearms pawn contracts and inventory were sold; the firearms ones were not | 🔴 **AFFECTS THE FIGURES** | The store's total inventory ≠ what was sold. The retained firearms assets either moved to another store's class or are still sitting in the sold store's class. Only the **non-firearms** portion comes off as sold |
| **F8** | **Goodwill basis is unknown.** $693,340 was allocated to goodwill. If it is **self-created**, basis is nil and the whole amount is gain. If Magnum **bought** this store, there may be amortisable §197 goodwill with remaining basis — and the amortisation already taken is **§1245 ordinary recapture**, not capital gain | 🔴 **AFFECTS THE FIGURES** | Read the 2024 depreciation/amortisation schedule for any §197 intangible tied to this location |
| **F9** | **Built-in gains tax (§1374) not yet ruled out.** A large gain in an S corporation that was **ever a C corporation** can carry entity-level tax | ⚖️ **TO CONFIRM** | Confirm the S election date and that there is no C-corp history. S from inception → §1374 cannot apply and the point closes |
| **F10** | **The Assignment of Lease carries no landlord signature in our copy** — the `ACCEPTANCE BY LANDLORD` block is blank | 🟡 **OPEN** | Does not change a figure by itself, but if the lease never validly assigned, the seller may still be on the lease. Affects whether the security deposit came back |
| **F11** | ⓘ The PSA spells the signing officer `Igor Melamud`; the Bill of Sale and allocation schedule spell it `Igor Melomed`. The premises ZIP on the PSA reads `33414` (Wellington) where the location is in the `333xx` Broward range | 🟢 **COSMETIC** | Noted only so a later reader does not treat them as two people or two places |
