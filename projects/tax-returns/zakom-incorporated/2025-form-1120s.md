| 🔑 **Retail purchase agreement / dealer invoice** | The sales document from the truck dealer | **Proves what the asset COST and when it was PLACED IN SERVICE** — the two inputs depreciation is built from. ⚠️ **A loan amount is NOT a cost**: cash or a trade-in may have gone to the dealer on top |
| 🔑 **Sale agreement** *(for something sold)* | The sales document, the other way round | **Proves the PROCEEDS.** Gain or loss = proceeds − *(cost − depreciation already taken)*. ⛔ **No document, no COMPUTABLE gain — the disposal is still reportable**, and §6 keeps Form 4797 open regardless |
| 🔑 **Insurance settlement letter** *(for something destroyed)* | The insurer's letter stating what it paid | **The proceeds figure for a total loss** — the sale agreement's equivalent when there is no buyer *(§4 decision 9)* |
| **The prior-year filed return** | Last year's finished 1120-S | 🔑 **The answer key.** The **opening balance sheet**, the **depreciation schedule every asset continues on**, and **the firm's own conventions** |

### ④ Why the truck documents matter more here than anywhere else

**A truck is not an expense. It is an asset**, deducted a piece at a time over several years
*(depreciation)*. To do that the return needs, **for every single vehicle**: what it cost · **when it
was placed in service** · how much has been deducted so far · and, when it leaves, what it sold for.

🔴 **And on this client none of that can be matched by NAME.** §3G ① establishes **two** naming
systems the client uses *(a description list and a unit-number list)* and **a third on the returns**
*(`#7107` / `#1800` / `HU4577`)*. ⚠️ **A possible fourth — `1838` on an interest letter — is flagged
by §3H ① as *"a fourth naming system, OR A TYPO. Ask,"* and is still open in §6.**

🔑 **The finance documents supply the VIN, which is why §3H ③ says the mapping should be asked for
against VINs rather than descriptions.** ⛔ **But a VIN is not a key all parties share — §3I ⑦
establishes that NEITHER FILED RETURN CARRIES ONE.** *(An earlier draft said it was; withdrawn.)*
