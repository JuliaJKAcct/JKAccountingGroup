### 🔑 The one thing a future session should read first

**Everything on this return hangs on a single unanswered fact: whether she has earned income in 2025.**

- **If she does** — then she can qualify for the **earned income credit** and the **additional child
  tax credit**, both of which *require* earned income, and the children become **worth a great deal
  on her return**. Head of household becomes worth having. The home office becomes deductible up to
  the §280A cap.
- **If she does not** — then the children are worth **nothing** to her beyond the Form 8962 family
  size, HOH is worth nothing either (there is no positive income for a bigger standard deduction to
  shelter), and the entire return is a **premium-tax-credit reconciliation**.

⚠️ **And the same is true of her ex-husband in the opposite direction:** for 2025 he has **no W-2 and
no Uber**, only an S-corporation **loss** — and a K-1 loss is **not earned income**. So on the
current picture **neither of them qualifies for a single refundable credit in 2025**, where in 2024
the household received **7,589** of them. **Her income is the only fact that can change that**, which
is why it is question 1 and not question 9.
