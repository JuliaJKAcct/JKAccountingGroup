## 4 · Findings — grouped by root cause, largest first

### Finding 1 · Company money held outside the company (USDT) — Undeposited Funds 44,889.19 at year end

- **What the books show.** Undeposited Funds went 363 → 44,889 over 2025 (37,259 Jun · 492 Jul ·
  61,935 Aug · 53,197 Oct · 10,096 Nov · 44,889 Dec) and keeps climbing in 2026: 88,311 Jan →
  **248,523.59 at 2026-08-31**.
- **What it is.** Customer payments in USDT into a cold wallet the owner holds ("not a wallet
  under my name or company, just a cold wallet" — owner, 2025-11-19). Recorded in QuickBooks as
  `Payment (USDT)` against invoices; cash arrives when the owner converts and deposits, minus a
  commission (252.28 on 10,000; 705 on 43,000; 426 and 319 booked as bank fees on other batches).
- **At 2025-12-31.** The GM reported to Julia on 2025-12-26 "for closing the books": **USDT
  pending 36,907.55**; the owner the same day: "around 43,450 pending". Julia's 2026-03-18 note:
  "a lot of open USDT payments from Diego since December"; the GM's report that day showed
  69,123.01 pending; the owner said he had 67,300 still to transfer.
- **Customers in these threads and their 2025 sales** (income-by-customer report): Alison Vanessa
  Gamboa 221,089.89 · Atias Holdings LLC 188,247.37 · Nexus Link LLC 150,380 · Infinite Wireless
  LLC 111,755 · Diego Lesmes 35,458 · Phone Center Pereira 20,436.66. Not all of it is USDT, but
  the channel is material to revenue.
- **Why it matters for the return.** Revenue is complete (invoices + payments recorded). The
  problem is the asset: at year end roughly 37k–43k of the company's sales proceeds were held by a
  shareholder, not by the company. That is a **receivable from the shareholder** (if it is his
  to remit) or a **distribution** (if it is not coming back), and the conversion fees are the
  company's expense only when the company bears them. A cash shortfall of 7,100 on a September
  batch was first booked against Stan Owner Capital, then partly moved to Sales (Maria, 2025-09-24);
  Stan Owner Capital still shows a −4,100 movement in September.
- **Also relevant.** The Florida DOR sales-tax audit (opened Mar 2026, records delivered Jun 2026)
  is looking at the cash deposits this produces (41,000 cash 2025-09-26; 63,800 in July).
- **Ask.** (a) The wallet ledger at 2025-12-31 — USDT received per customer, converted, deposited,
  fees; (b) Julia's call on P4; (c) a corporate USDT/exchange account (Julia proposed one
  2026-03-18; the GM doubted it solves the conversion step).
