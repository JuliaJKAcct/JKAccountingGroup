## 0 · The year in one paragraph

2025 is the **first full year** of the company's two managed brokerage accounts at J.P. Morgan
Securities, opened **6 December 2024**. The shape of the investment return changed completely
between the two years: 2024 earned **interest** on cash and CDs and the portfolio lost value on
paper; 2025 earned essentially **no interest at all** — the fixed-income holdings are funds, which
pay dividends — and the portfolio **gained** substantially on paper while **realising a capital
loss**. Both facts matter for the return, and they pull in opposite directions on Schedule M-1.
The equity account (…1499) was **largely rebuilt in late November 2025**, which is relevant to the
dividends-received deduction (§4, decision D3).
