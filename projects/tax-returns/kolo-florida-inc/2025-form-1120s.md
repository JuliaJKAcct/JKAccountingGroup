## 0 · The year in one paragraph

**A wind-down.** Kolo sells bath and shower fixtures, mostly **off** its Shopify storefront — the
store took fewer than 50 orders all year while the warehouse held over eleven thousand units. The
company **closed in 2026**, and its QuickBooks was disconnected with it, so **every figure below
was exported by hand before the books became unreachable.** 2025 is a normal (not final) year:
the **final return will be 2026**. Rent is roughly four fifths of every expense and more than
doubled; the loss is essentially the lease. Payroll stopped, officer compensation is nil, and one
of the two shareholders left — his 2024 K-1 was marked `Final`, so **2025 has one shareholder at
100%**.

⚠️ **2024 was a SHORT first year** (30 May – 31 Dec 2024), which is why its opening balance sheet
is all zeros and why its conventions had to be decoded rather than assumed.
